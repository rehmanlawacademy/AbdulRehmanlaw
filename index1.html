Free Online Tool

Check Your Active Taxpayer List (ATL) Status

Being on the FBR Active Taxpayer List (Income Tax) means lower withholding tax, no surcharge on banking transactions, and eligibility for government tenders. Verify your status instantly on the official FBR IRIS portal.

  • Lower withholding tax rates on payments
  • No 2.5% ATL surcharge on banking
  • Required for tenders & imports

Official FBR Verification

Active Taxpayer List (Income Tax)

Check Income Tax Active Taxpayer Status List

  1. 1Open the official FBR IRIS 2.0 verification portal
  2. 2Scroll to Online Verifications section
  3. 3Select Active Taxpayer List (Income Tax)
  4. 4Enter your CNIC / NTN, fill captcha & verify
Check ATL Status on FBR IRIS

You will be redirected to the official Government of Pakistan FBR portal (iris.fbr.gov.pk).
